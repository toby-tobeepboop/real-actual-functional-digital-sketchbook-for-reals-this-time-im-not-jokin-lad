My Reflection

I feel like a lot could've gone better with this, and I broke about a million things before I finally found a way for (almost) everything to work nearly identically to the jpg provided. I found a way to make the borders work, not knowing exactly how to make them fill the whole page, and I spend a billion ages trying to figure out how to make all the hyperlinks different sizes and colors. I learnt that real cool css takes a lot of broken and terrible css before anything can ever look good ever. With more time, I'd love to fix the text colors at the bottom of the page- but I think if I look at the css for this page any longer, my head will explode off my shoulders. But that is how you know the challenge is worthwhile, I think. The mkst difficult to reproduce by far was the hyperlinks all being slightly different. I want to learn more about that sort of stylization- but for now, I'll stick to image buttons in my personal projects.
